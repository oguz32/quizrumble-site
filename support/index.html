Hosting a quiz

Tap Host a quiz, pick categories (or your own questions, or questions from the shared library), the number of questions and the time per question, then Open the room. Players join with the room code and password, or by scanning the QR code on your screen. Keep the app open on your phone while the quiz runs: your phone runs the game.

Joining a quiz

Tap Join a quiz, scan the host's QR code or type the room code and password, and pick a nickname. Stay in the app during the quiz: if you switch to another app, take a screenshot or record the screen, you are removed from the game.

Venue only rooms

A host can require players to be nearby. You will be asked for your location when you join; it is only compared with the host's position.

Pro subscription

Pro raises rooms to 100 players and removes ads for your players. Manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. If it doesn't show after a purchase, use Restore purchases on the Pro screen.

Reporting a question

In the question library, tap the flag next to a question to report it. Questions with several reports are hidden.
